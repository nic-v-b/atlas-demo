FIRST-DAY INVESTOR'S GUIDE

What do these words mean?

What is adjusted cost base (ACB)?

Think of ACB as the tax-record cost of an investment. For identical taxable shares, divide the pooled purchase cost, including applicable fees, by the combined shares to find the average cost per share. A sale uses that average to allocate cost and calculate a preliminary gain or loss.

Why are TFSA shares handled differently?

Shares inside a TFSA do not join your ordinary taxable ACB pool. However, transactions in registered accounts can still matter when investigating superficial-loss rules after a loss sale in a taxable account.

What is a superficial loss?

A loss on a taxable sale may not be deductible if you or an affiliated person acquire the same or an identical investment within the 30-day window around the sale and still hold substituted property 30 days afterward.
